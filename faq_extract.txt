BUSINESS ADVISORY
28. How is business advisory different from legal advisory?
Legal advisory primarily addresses rights, obligations, regulations, contracts and legal risks. Business advisory considers broader commercial and operational implications. Where appropriate, Astronis Global integrates these perspectives so that advice also considers practical implementation and business objectives.
29. Can you help businesses develop a regulatory strategy before launching a new service?
Yes. Early regulatory planning can be particularly important for businesses operating in regulated industries. We can assess the proposed business model, identify key regulatory considerations and assist in developing an appropriate implementation and compliance roadmap.
